Our Story

From a Small Corner
to Your Neighborhood

What started as a tiny espresso bar with three stools and a dream has grown into two beloved locations across the city. We still roast our own beans, bake our own bread, and hand-craft every drink that leaves our counter.

Our custom cakes have become the talk of every celebration — from first birthdays to golden anniversaries. Each one is designed with you, baked by us, and remembered by everyone who takes a bite.

Two outlets. One commitment: fresh ingredients, zero shortcuts, and a warm welcome every time you walk through our doors.
